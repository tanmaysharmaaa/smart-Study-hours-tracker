# Project Title

Smart Study Hours Tracker

## Problem Statement

Many students struggle to manage their study time effectively. They often don't know how many hours they spend on each subject, making it difficult to stay consistent and improve their productivity.

## Proposed Solution

A web application that allows students to record their daily study sessions, track study hours, monitor progress, and maintain study streaks. The application will provide simple reports and charts to help students analyze their study habits.

## Key Features

- Daily Study Session Logging
- Subject-wise Study Hours
- Weekly & Monthly Progress Reports
- Study Streak Tracking
- Charts & Analytics Dashboard
